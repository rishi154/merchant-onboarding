# Workflow Routing Test Scenarios

## LOW Risk → Express Workflow (4 agents)
**Upload these documents:**
- Simple business license (clear, standard format)
- Basic bank statement (1-2 pages, clear transactions)
- Standard ID document

**Expected Risk Assessment Results:**
- Low financial risk
- Standard business structure
- Clear documentation
- Risk Tier: LOW

## MEDIUM Risk → Standard Workflow (7 agents)  
**Upload these documents:**
- Business license with some complexity
- Bank statements (3-5 pages with mixed transactions)
- Multiple ID documents
- Basic financial statements

**Expected Risk Assessment Results:**
- Moderate financial risk
- Some complexity in business structure
- Generally clear documentation
- Risk Tier: MEDIUM

## HIGH Risk → Comprehensive Workflow (14 agents)
**Upload these documents:**
- Complex business structure documents
- Extensive bank statements (6+ pages)
- Multiple entity documents
- Complex financial statements
- International transaction records

**Expected Risk Assessment Results:**
- High financial risk
- Complex business structure
- Multiple entities or jurisdictions
- Risk Tier: HIGH
